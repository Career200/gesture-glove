# notes

Scratch. Unsorted thoughts, hunches, half-arguments and things noticed in passing.

**Nothing here is settled.** `docs/` holds what has been measured or decided; this folder holds
what hasn't. Expect contradictions, expect things to be wrong, expect to delete half of it. Items
graduate into `docs/` when they've earned it, or get struck through when they haven't.

Rough dates on entries so it's possible to tell later what was known when.
